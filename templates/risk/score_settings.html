How to use this page

Change any value on the left and click Preview. You will see every student's score now and with your values, and who would change colour. Nothing is saved until you click Save.

Why these starting values: missing work is the strongest sign that a student is in trouble, so it has the largest share. A long gap between meetings comes next, because the course makes a weekly meeting mandatory. The other three are earlier and weaker signals.
